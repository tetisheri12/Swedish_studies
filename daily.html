How this works

Built on the Fluent Forever method. Sounds first: a short ear game for Swedish sounds. Spaced repetition: every card comes back after 1, 3, 9 and 21 days, 2 months and 6 months — just before it would be forgotten. Only what you don’t know: each station starts with a quick check where you tap the words you don’t know. Pictures and personal links: for each new word, look at pictures (🔍) and write your own memory hook. Grammar through sentences: the grammar game drills the pattern with many short sentences. Real Swedish: a video per station. Output: say and write something every day; a parent corrects it and the corrected sentence becomes a card.

The stations follow Rivstart A1+A2 (chapters 3–20), Rivstart B1+B2 (1–18) and Rivstart B2+C1 (1–12). Word lists and glosses are written for this site; the books stay with you.
