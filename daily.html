How this works

Based on the Fluent Forever method: words come with pictures instead of English, he must try to remember before seeing the answer, and each card comes back after 1, 3, 9 and 21 days, 2 months and 6 months — just before it would be forgotten. A missed card starts again. The daily sound game trains the ear for Swedish sounds that English and Turkish don't have. Sentences from finished missions, words saved in the Reader and your corrected sentences become cards automatically.
