Reference & grown-up controls

The rules are here when you need them.

Start with a mission. Open a rule only when it helps. Accept a well-explained alternative answer in conversation; tile puzzles use one supplied model.

Run a short lesson together

Choose one mission. Let the learner attempt it before explaining. Ask “What did you notice?” after a correction. Finish by acting out the situation with invented details. Revisit the audio separately with a passage you have checked.
