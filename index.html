Reference & grown-up controls

The rules are here when you need them.

Start with a mission. Open a rule only when it helps. Accept a well-explained alternative answer in conversation; tile puzzles use one supplied model.

Voice for 🔊 buttons

Uses the Swedish voice built into this device. On iPad/Mac: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Swedish, and download a better voice.

Run a short lesson together

Choose one mission. Let the learner attempt it before explaining. Ask “What did you notice?” after a correction. Finish by acting out the situation with invented details. Suggested order per mission: Words (hear and repeat) → Story (read along, then read alone) → Challenges → Speak.

Progress, privacy & publishing

Mission progress and the Unit 6 room save in this browser under a separate key from your original site. No account, analytics, database, or external libraries. Storage can be blocked by browser settings. Export a backup if your progress matters.
